# 🌾 Grain Island

A charming 2D farming simulator game built with C# in Unity. Manage your own island farm, grow crops, raise animals, and build a thriving community.

---

## 📖 About the Project

**Grain Island** is an indie farming simulation game where players can experience the peaceful art of farming. Cultivate diverse crops, care for livestock, and create the ultimate island paradise. Whether you're a casual gamer seeking relaxation or a farming enthusiast looking for depth, Grain Island offers an engaging and immersive farming experience.

### Game Highlights

- 🌱 **Diverse Crop System** – Plant and harvest a variety of crops with realistic growing mechanics
- 🐑 **Animal Husbandry** – Raise and care for livestock to produce valuable resources
- 🛠️ **Building & Customization** – Design and expand your farm with custom structures and decorations
- 🌍 **Island Exploration** – Discover hidden areas and unlock new farming opportunities
- 💰 **Economic Simulation** – Manage resources, trade goods, and grow your farm business
- 🎨 **Charming Aesthetics** – Hand-crafted pixel art and cozy atmosphere
